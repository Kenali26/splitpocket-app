# SplitPocket

**Split bills with friends, settle up fairly, and keep a monthly budget.**

👉 **Open the app: [splitpocket.pkg4.win](https://splitpocket.pkg4.win/)** (free, works in any browser on phone or computer)

## What you can do

- **Split bills** for trips, roommates, dinners and groups: equally, by exact amounts, by shares or by percentage
- **Settle up in the fewest payments.** SplitPocket works out who pays whom.
- **Share a split with a link** so everyone can add expenses together, live
- **Chat** with the people in each split
- **Track a monthly budget** with spending categories and over-budget alerts. Your share of shared bills is added automatically.
- **Sign in with Google or email.** Your data syncs across devices and works offline.

Supports USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD and AED.
